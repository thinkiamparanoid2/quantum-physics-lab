Is it right?

How it is checked

Every simulation is exact or checked against an independent method: a textbook formula, a second numerical method, or numpy, Qiskit and PennyLane in the Python lab. These 81 automated checks (some run once per reference case) run on every change.

Wave mechanics 6

Eigenstates, split-operator evolution, shooting and transmission against exact results.

  • FFT matches a direct DFT and inverts
  • particle in a box: E_n = n^2 pi^2 / (2 L^2), orthonormal states with n-1 nodes
  • harmonic oscillator: E_n = n + 1/2, and the shooting method agrees
  • Gaussian packet: minimum uncertainty, then free spreading as sigma(t) = sigma sqrt(1 + (t / 2 sigma^2)^2)
  • a coherent state in the oscillator swings like a classical ball without spreading
  • transmission matches the exact rectangular-barrier formula, below and above the top

Spin and atoms 9

Stern–Gerlach, magnetic resonance and hydrogen.

  • measuring spin along a tilted axis gives cos^2(theta/2)
  • Stern-Gerlach chains: z then z keeps everything, z x z brings spin down back
  • magnetic resonance: exact rotating-field solution matches direct integration and Rabi
  • spin echo: spread-out spins refocus at twice the pulse spacing
  • hydrogen radial functions are normalised, orthogonal and give <r> = (3n^2 - l(l+1))/2
  • real spherical harmonics are orthonormal on the sphere
  • the radial Schrodinger equation, solved numerically, gives E = -1/(2 n^2)
  • hydrogen spectrum: energies and the Balmer lines (vacuum wavelengths)
  • sampled electron clouds follow |psi|^2

The founding experiments 5

Photoelectric effect, double slit, Mach–Zehnder, bomb tester and Compton scattering.

  • photoelectric effect: E = hf, threshold, stopping voltage and Millikan's h/e
  • double slit: fringe spacing lambda L / d, marked paths add intensities
  • Mach-Zehnder matches the same circuit built from gates: Rx(-pi/2), phase, Rx(-pi/2)
  • bomb tester: a live bomb is found without exploding a quarter of the time; Zeno pushes it to 1
  • Compton scattering: shift h/mc (1 - cos theta), with energy and momentum both conserved

Nonlocality, noise and error correction 5

CHSH, density matrices, decoherence and the repetition and Shor codes.

  • CHSH: classical strategies top out at 3/4, quantum reaches 1/2 + sqrt2/4
  • density matrices: Bloch round trip, purity, entropy, partial trace of a Bell pair
  • decoherence: each environment qubit multiplies the coherence by cos(angle/2)
  • repetition code: 3p^2 - 2p^3, and bigger codes help only below p = 1/2
  • Shor's code corrects any single-qubit error, including arbitrary rotations

Many particles and approximations 5

Bands, identical particles, perturbation theory, the variational method and WKB.

  • Kronig-Penney bands agree with the levels of a long chain of wells
  • identical particles: fermions avoid each other, bosons bunch, same state forbidden for fermions
  • perturbation theory: a linear field on the oscillator shifts every level by -F^2/2 at second order
  • variational method: exact for the oscillator, an upper bound for the quartic well
  • WKB: exact for the oscillator, close for a V-shaped well, and a tunnelling estimate
