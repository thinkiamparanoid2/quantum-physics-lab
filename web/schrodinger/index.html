Schrödinger Playground

Type or draw any potential, see its energy levels, and watch a particle move in it.

Syntax
  • 0.5*x^2 use x, numbers, + − * / and ^ for powers
  • 6*(abs(x) > 2) comparisons count as 1 (true) or 0 (false)
  • sin cos exp log sqrt abs tanh min max step and pi

Units: ħ = m = 1. Hard walls stand at x = ±10.

Drag across the top plot to draw V(x). Press Draw it again when done.

Click levels in Energy levels to add or remove them. They start with equal weights.

The potential is sampled on 700 points between hard walls at x = ±10. The lowest 110 stationary states come from diagonalising the finite-difference Hamiltonian, and the motion is the exact sum of those states with their phases e−iEnt. "Captured" says how much of the starting state those 110 levels describe.
