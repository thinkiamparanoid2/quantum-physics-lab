Three ideas, and you can follow everything on this site

You don't need advanced maths. Each lesson builds on these three, and the visuals do most of the explaining.

α|0⟩ + β|1⟩

A qubit is not just 0 or 1

A bit is either 0 or 1. A qubit carries two amplitudes, α and β, and the squares of their sizes are the chances of reading 0 or 1. Until you measure, both possibilities are in play.

↗ + ↙ = 0

Amplitudes can cancel

Probabilities only ever add up. Amplitudes are arrows with a direction, called the phase, so they can also cancel. Quantum algorithms arrange that cancellation so wrong answers disappear.

|00⟩ + |11⟩

Qubits can share one state

Entangled qubits have no separate states of their own. Measure one and you learn about the other at once. That shared state is the resource behind teleportation and quantum cryptography.
